A mental math game for the whole family, played together on the couch. Everyone joins a room from their own phone, then plays five bursts of four questions each, answered in private. Streaks multiply the score, and between bursts each player's level adjusts quietly, so a kid and a grown-up can play in the same game and both be challenged. Levels are only revealed at the end.
