The whole thing started on the `tlapalli-vscode-theme` repo. I originally only intended to have a single monochromatic theme. But then it all spiraled out into what it is today.

I am quite satisfied with the outcome, and perhaps it isn't the most accessible theme, nor as polished as if it came from a design studio, but I do pride myself on caring a lot about what goes into my work and strive to provide the best quality I can. If you ever try it I would appreciate a review, any feedback and maybe a rating if you don't mind!
