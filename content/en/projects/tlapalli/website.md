A website with a special theme picker. Sound effects and haptics for mobile. It changes between dark, light, and system mode for the 16 variations. A carefully crafted glass effect on the nav bar as well as the responsiveness and scroll transitions on the page.

I wanted to share the different variations in a more interactive way than just the README. So I thought I could probably create a landing page for it, similar to what Ayu does for example. And thus the website was born.
